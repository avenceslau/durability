---
'durability': minor
---

`DurabilitySemaphore` and `DurabilitySemaphoreQueue` take a `channel` option, so one object can hold several semaphore pools and several queues. Each pool has its own limit, groups, rounds, grants, and leases, reached through its own application RPC method. Each queue scopes its items, grants, keys, and leases, so queues may reuse item IDs and keys. Requests and syncs gain an optional `clientChannel`: the holder counts a client as `clientId` plus `clientChannel`, so two queues sharing a `clientId` and a pool cannot claim, release, or settle each other's permits, and it echoes a sent `clientChannel` in its response. A named queue applies nothing from a holder that does not echo its channel and asks it for no leases, so a holder that predates channels never admits its work; upgrade holders first. The default queue keeps its wire format and its `durability.semaphore-queue` outbox channel, and a named queue's outbox channel is a JSON tuple. Migrations move existing grants, leases, items, keys, and pending reports to the default channel without changing their IDs; records of a channel no instance attached are kept untouched; downgrading refuses to run while non-default records exist. Lease and `grant_reclaimed` lifecycle events gain `clientChannel`.
