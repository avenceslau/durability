---
'durability': minor
---

`DurabilitySemaphore` and `DurabilitySemaphoreQueue` take a `channel` option, so one object can hold several semaphore pools and several queues. Each pool has its own limit, groups, rounds, grants, and leases, reached through its own application RPC method. Each queue scopes its items, grants, keys, and leases, so queues may reuse item IDs and keys. Default queues keep version-1 messages; named queues use version 2 with `clientChannel`. The holder counts a client as `clientId` plus `clientChannel`, so queues sharing a `clientId` and a pool cannot claim, release, or settle each other's permits. It echoes the version and channel in its response. Old holders reject version 2 before allocating grants or leases, and named queues retain their work until a compatible holder responds; upgrade holders first. The default queue keeps its wire format and its `durability.semaphore-queue` outbox channel, and a named queue's outbox channel is a JSON tuple. Migrations move existing grants, leases, items, keys, and pending reports to the default channel without changing their IDs; records of a channel no instance attached are kept untouched; downgrading refuses to run while non-default records exist. Lease and `grant_reclaimed` lifecycle events gain optional `clientChannel` metadata.
